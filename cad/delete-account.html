Delete your Emuverse 3D CAD account and data

This page explains how to delete your account and the data linked to it in Emuverse 3D CAD (Google Play: "Emuverse: SKP, DWG & 3D Viewer", package studio.emuverse.cad3d), published by Emuverse Studio.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Emuverse 3D CAD.
  2. Tap the menu button (☰) at the top left, then Profile.
  3. Scroll down to Delete account and tap Delete account and data.
  4. Confirm with Delete permanently.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email to support@emuverse.studio from the Google account you used, with the subject "3D CAD Account Deletion". We complete the request within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • The link between the app and your Google account, and your profile details stored on your phone (name, email address, profile picture).
  • All backups the app saved in the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive, and the app's permission to use that folder. Your other Drive files are never touched. (You can also remove this data yourself in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Emuverse 3D CAD → Delete hidden app data.)

What is kept, and why

  • Free trial record: our server never stores your name or email address. It keeps only a one-way, salted hash of your Google account identifier and of your device identifier, with the trial dates, so that the free trial cannot be repeated. This record cannot be turned back into your account and is kept for fraud prevention while the app offers the free trial.
  • Drawings and projects on your phone are not part of your account. They stay on your device until you remove them from the app's list or uninstall the app.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. To stop future charges, cancel it first in the app (Store → Manage subscription) or in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
