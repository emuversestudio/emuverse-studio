Privacy Policy for Emuverse Retro Games

Last updated: May 3, 2026

At Emuverse Retro Games, accessible from emuverse.studio, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Emuverse and how we use it.

1. Data Collection

Emuverse Retro Games does not collect any personal data or private information from its users. The application operates primarily locally on your device to ensure maximum privacy.

2. Permissions

The app requires access to your device's storage solely to scan and load game ROM files. This data is processed only on your device and is never uploaded to any external servers or shared with third parties.

3. Advertisements and Payments

We use Google AdMob for advertisements and Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. These services may collect device identifiers and anonymized usage data to provide their services under their own respective privacy policies.

4. Local Storage

Emuverse stores only your preferences (volume settings, scanned folder paths, save states) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you manually export or move your files.

5. ROM Files

Emuverse is an emulator only and does not include any game ROM files. Users are solely responsible for the ROM files they load. You must own the original software or have the legal right to use any ROM files you play.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: support@emuverse.studio
