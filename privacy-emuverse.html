Privacy Policy for Emuverse Retro Games

Last updated: May 3, 2024

At Emuverse Retro Games, accessible from emuverse.studio, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Emuverse and how we use it.

1. Data Collection

Emuverse Retro Games does not collect any personal data or private information from its users. The application operates locally on your device.

2. Permissions

The app requires access to your device's storage to scan and load game ROM files. This data is processed only on the device and is never uploaded to any external server.

3. Advertisements and Payments

We use Google AdMob for ads and Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. These services may collect device identifiers to provide their services under their own privacy policies.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions, contact us at: support@emuverse.studio
