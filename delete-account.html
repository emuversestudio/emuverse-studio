Delete your Emuverse Retro Games account and data

This page explains how to delete your account and the data linked to it in Emuverse Retro Games (package studio.emuverse.retro), published by Emuverse Studio. Looking for Emuverse 3D CAD? See its account deletion page.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Emuverse Retro Games and open the menu.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete my account.
  4. Optionally tick Also delete my save backups in Google Drive, then confirm with Delete.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email to support@emuverse.studio from the Google account you used, with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We complete the request within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your Emuverse account (Firebase Authentication user) and the email address linked to it.
  • Your cloud saves, play statistics and Premium record stored on our servers, and the purchase index entries that point to your account.
  • If you choose it, the save backups the app stored in the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive.

What is kept, and why

  • Order-level revenue records that contain no personal data are kept for accounting.
  • Games and saves on your phone are not part of your account. They stay on your device until you remove them or uninstall the app.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. To stop future charges, cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions (the app shows a "Manage subscriptions" button before you confirm).
